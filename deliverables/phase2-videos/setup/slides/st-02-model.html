The mental model

One binary. Three directories.

./ckptfs  /mnt/aifs  /tmp/aifs-backend  /mnt/nvme/aifs-cache
Mountpoint
The virtual filesystem your apps use. Nothing is stored here.
Lower — durable backend
NFS / ANF in production. Source of truth after a crash.
Spool — local NVMe
Every new write lands here first, at NVMe speed.
write → spool (instant ack) → background replication → lower → .aifs_committed
Reads resolve spool-first, then fall back to the backend
